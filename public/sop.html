The 10-Minute SLA AI Pipeline

Data Routing & LLM Processing Infrastructure

Node 1: Ingestion (The Catch)

Standard agencies run Meta Ads and drop leads into a hidden CSV file or a slow email notification. The Med Spa owner's front desk manually checks this hours later. The lead dies here.

Our infrastructure bypasses the human element. A dedicated webhook listens directly to the Meta Ads API. The exact second a prospect submits a lead form, the raw JSON payload is caught and pushed into our processing layer.

Node 2: Neural Net Processor (The Brain)

The payload is instantly routed to the OpenAI (gpt-4o) API. We utilize a strict system prompt that acts as the clinic's elite concierge. The LLM algorithmically parses the data to:

  • Identify the requested service (e.g., Botox, Lip Fillers, Laser).
  • Score the prospect's intent level from 1-10 based on text inputs.
  • Draft a highly empathetic, custom SMS reply designed specifically to elicit a calendar booking.

Node 3: Automated Execution (The Strike)

The AI-drafted response is pushed via API directly into the clinic's CRM (e.g., GoHighLevel). The system automatically builds the contact profile, tags them as 'High Intent', and fires the SMS to the prospect's phone.

Performance Metric: Total execution time from ad-click to custom SMS delivery is strictly under 10 seconds. Speed-to-lead is driven to zero.

Master Closing SOP

C.L.O.S.E.R. Methodology + A.A.A. Objection Handling

The Core Sales Philosophy

  • The Diagnostician: The person asking the questions controls the frame. Never monologue. Never defend. You are diagnosing a broken business.
  • Sell the Vacation: Do not sell the flight. Med Spa owners do not care about APIs or webhooks; they care about high-ticket bookings magically appearing on their calendar.
  • Conviction Closes: The prospect must believe in your certainty more than their own doubt. You are selling a solution that fixes their primary bottleneck.

Step 1: (C) Clarify & (L) Label

Extract their pain and summarize it so accurately that they instantly trust you.

"What made you take the time to jump on this call today? What is your monthly revenue sitting at, and where do you want it to be in 12 months?"

"So, let me make sure I'm hearing this right. You guys offer amazing services, but right now, you're relying on your front desk to manually call back leads. Because of that delay, high-ticket leads are going cold and booking with the clinic down the street. Is that an accurate label?"

Step 2: (O) Overview & (S) Sell the Vacation

Twist the knife on past failures, then present the Veluse Infrastructure.

"What have you tried in the past to fix this? Have you hired marketing agencies before? How much time and money have you wasted trying to figure this out?"

"The problem isn't your ads. The problem is your data routing. If a Med Spa lead isn't contacted in under 5 minutes, they are gone. We install the Veluse Algorithmic Pipeline. When a lead clicks your ad, our system qualifies them and instantly sends an AI-drafted SMS to book them on your calendar. Your front desk does nothing."

Step 3: (E) Explain Away Concerns (A.A.A. Protocol)

Never argue. Agree and redirect using Acknowledge, Associate, Ask.

Objection: "I need to think about it."
Acknowledge: "I hear you, it's smart to think through big decisions."
Associate: "The best Med Spa owners we work with never rush into anything."
Ask: "Usually when people need to think about it, it means I failed to explain something clearly. What's the biggest question mark still sitting in your head right now?"

Meta Ads Playbook

Interruption Traffic & Creative Strategy

Creative is the New Targeting

We no longer rely on hyper-specific interest targeting. We use Open Targeting (Advantage+ Audiences) and let Meta's AI find the buyers. Your creative is your targeting.

Use the 4 Angles: Pain-driven, Proof-driven, Identity-driven, or Value-driven. Do not mix angles in a single ad.

The "Hook Rate" KPI

The hook (first 3 seconds of a video) dictates success. 90% of users drop off if the hook fails.

Hook Rate Formula: (3-Second Video Plays ÷ Impressions).
If the Hook Rate is below 25%, kill the ad or change the first 3 seconds.

Campaign Structure: Test vs. Scale

  • Testing Campaign: Low budget. Used exclusively to test new visual and verbal hooks. Run for 3-7 days to gather data.
  • Scaling Campaign: High budget using Campaign Budget Optimization (CBO). Only proven, winning ads from the testing phase graduate to this campaign. Never scale by more than 20% every 48 hours to avoid resetting the learning phase.

Google Ads Playbook

Search & High-Intent Traffic Capture

Bottom-of-Funnel Search Strategy

While Meta interrupts, Google intercepts. When a prospect searches "Lip fillers near me", they are ready to buy. We must dominate local search radius visibility.

  • Keyword Match Types: Do not use Broad Match for local Med Spas. Stick strictly to Phrase Match ("Botox clinic") and Exact Match ([Botox near me]) to protect the budget from wasted clicks.
  • Negative Keywords: Review the Search Terms report every 48 hours. Aggressively block terms like "cheap", "free", "training", and "DIY".

The 30/30/30 Ad Copy Rule

Great Google Search Ads must balance three elements perfectly:

  • 30% Keyword Focus: Putting the exact search term in the headline.
  • 30% Call to Action: Clear directives (e.g., "Book Free Consult").
  • 30% Emotional Triggers: (e.g., "Reclaim Your Youth").

Landing Page Congruency (CRO)

Never send paid search traffic to a generic homepage. If the ad promotes CoolSculpting, the link must route to a dedicated CoolSculpting landing page that reiterates the exact offer promised in the ad. This improves Google Quality Score, lowers Cost Per Click (CPC), and massively increases the conversion rate.
